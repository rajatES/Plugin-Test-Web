    How to test

    1. Load an NFL or NBA article: import a link or paste the text.
    2. Tick its league under Categories.
    3. Click Find stat add-ons below the article. It takes about a minute.
    4. Insert the add-ons you like, edit their text, then click Preview to see the article as readers would.

    Only the NFL and NBA are covered for now.
